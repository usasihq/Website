# Review of "The Titans of Code: The Top 100 Open Source Developers" (PDF supplied by the owner, 2026-10-01)

The report is titled "Top 100" but contains 26 profiles (many pages are blank). It is not a primary
source and was not cited anywhere on the site. Personal details it includes (birthplaces, birth years,
nationalities) were not used.

## Accuracy problems found

Verified on 2026-10-01 against the person's or organization's own pages:

- Chris Lattner: lists Lattner as Modular's CEO; Modular's 2026-07-29 announcement says Lattner will take on the role
  of Executive Vice President of Advanced AI Software and Platforms after Qualcomm's acquisition.
- Soumith Chintala: "VP at Meta AI" is outdated; soumith.ch says Chintala was at Meta for 11 years and
  is now at Thinking Machines and NYU. Torch7 is listed as a "seminal creation"; soumith.ch says
  Chintala maintained Torch7.
- François Chollet: "Researcher at Google" is outdated; fchollet.com lists co-founder of Ndea and ARC
  Prize and Keras creator/project lead (2015–present).

Likely errors based on widely documented history (not re-verified on 2026-10-01; none of this is
used on the site):

- Chris Lattner: says LLVM began as an undergraduate project; it began as graduate research at UIUC.
- Salvatore Sanfilippo: "stepped down from Redis maintenance" is outdated (returned to Redis in late 2024).
- Guido van Rossum: GIL removal (PEP 703) is attributed to Guido; PEP 703 was authored by Sam Gross.
- Bjarne Stroustrup: "directing research at Morgan Stanley" is outdated (left Morgan Stanley in 2022).
- Theo de Raadt: DARPA funding was withdrawn in 2003, not 2000; the OpenBSD Foundation was formed in
  2007; OpenBSD did not pioneer ASLR.
- David S. Miller: eBPF's architect was Alexei Starovoitov; credited CUBIC work is not Miller's.
- Greg Kroah-Hartman: not the "sole" stable maintainer (co-maintained with Sasha Levin).
- Fabrice Bellard: "MicroLLM" could not be found; Bellard's published LLM work is TextSynth/ts_server
  and ts_zip.
- Marshall Kirk McKusick: the BSD daemon artwork is credited to John Lasseter, not McKusick.
- Several superlatives and unsourced figures ("most popular", "70,000 commits", "solitary gatekeeper").

## People Behind Local AI decisions

Fit test: public work that helps others run AI models on their own hardware, and a link to a
published catalog record.

- Added: Chris Lattner (Modular: MAX local serving, Mojo), Soumith Chintala (PyTorch, ExecuTorch),
  François Chollet (Keras/KerasHub open-weight presets). All facts re-sourced from primary pages.
- Not added: Fabrice Bellard (local-AI work, but no catalog record to link and not U.S.-governed);
