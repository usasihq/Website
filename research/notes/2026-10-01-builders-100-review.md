# Review of "100 Open Source Builders" (research edition dated 2 October 2026; PDF supplied by the owner)

A careful, source-cited reference: 100 numbered profiles, each with biography, history, contributions,
context, and linked sources. It explicitly avoids overstating individual credit and notes licensing
caveats. It is not cited on the site; every fact used on the site was re-sourced from primary pages.

## Accuracy

Profiles read in full: 001–002, 032–035, 071, 091–100 (every profile relevant to People Behind Local
AI, plus a sample). No factual errors were found in them. Claims checked against primary pages on
2026-10-01 agree with the report, including: Chollet (fchollet.com roles; Keras 3 backends),
Chintala (soumith.ch affiliations), Gerganov (ggml.ai acquisition by Hugging Face; 2026-02-20
announcement), Wolf (Transformers first commit and paper), Chaumond (hf CLI post, CTO title),
Karpathy (karpathy.ai roles and dates), Howard (Answer.AI and fast.ai), and DHH (Omarchy News post of
2026-09-23 announcing Omacom's sponsorship of 0xSero, byline "By DHH"). The other ~75 profiles were not
checked in depth.

## People Behind Local AI decisions

sponsorship), François Chollet, Soumith Chintala, Georgi Gerganov.

Added: Thomas Wolf, Julien Chaumond, Andrej Karpathy, Jeremy Howard, and 0xSero (named in the DHH
profile's sources; handle only).
