## Findings for editors (not acted on; outside this assignment)

1. **NIST renaming.** Under EO 14434, NIST pages now use "super intelligence". Old AI URLs redirect, for example `/artificial-intelligence/ai-standards` now goes to `/super-intelligence/super-intelligence-standards`. Catalog source URLs on `nist.gov` may start redirecting. The `nist` record already notes the CAISSI name change.
2. **The MCP record's versioning link** (`https://modelcontextprotocol.io/specification/versioning`) now redirects to `/docs/2026-07-28/learn/versioning`. The content is unchanged.
3. **Codex CLI record.** The sandbox documentation says the `untrusted` approval policy is retired. That may be worth a line if the record ever mentions approval modes; it currently does not.
4. **Isaac ROS tutorial links are broken.** The Isaac Lab gear-assembly tutorial links to Isaac ROS sim-to-real tutorials, and both linked URLs return 404 on `nvidia-isaac-ros.github.io` today. The explainer does not rely on them.
5. **openpi has no documented physical safety procedures** in the README or example files I read. The openpi record makes no safety claims, which is correct.
6. **Methodology versus this assignment.** The methodology says the catalog does not publish power-capacity figures. The assignment allowed exactly quoted figures with caveats. I quoted one renewable-generation MW figure and withheld the CoreWeave MW figures; see the note under Infrastructure source 4.
7. **A planned `ai-rmf` artifact is missing.** The round-3 brief lists an `ai-rmf` artifact slug, but no such record exists. The policy explainer links to the NIST and Dioptra records instead.
