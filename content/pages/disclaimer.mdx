Nothing on this site is an official statement, record, approval, or designation.

USASI is also not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by any organization, project, or person it lists. The site's use of the words "United States of America" describes its subject — American AI work — and does not suggest government origin.

<h2 id="not-certification">Not a safety, security, or quality certification</h2>

The catalog does not test, audit, certify, or rank any model, product, dataset, or tool. It does not assess whether any system is safe, secure, accurate, reliable, or fit for a purpose. Openness labels such as open-weight, open-stack, and fully open are USASI editorial categories (rubric v0.1) describing documented materials and licenses; they are not quality or safety ratings and are not issued by any standards body.

<h2 id="names">Names and trademarks</h2>

Organization names, product names, and project names are used nominatively — only to identify the organizations and works the catalog describes. They remain the property of their owners. USASI does not display scraped logos; entries use plain text monograms instead. No listed organization has reviewed or approved how it is described here unless an entry explicitly says so with a source.

<h2 id="accuracy">Accuracy and limitations</h2>

The catalog aims to be accurate and to cite its sources, but it can be incomplete or out of date. Organizations change products, licenses, structures, and locations, and linked pages can change or disappear after they were reviewed. Each entry shows the date its evidence was last reviewed. "Unknown" means the catalog has not assessed something or lacks sufficient evidence; a count of zero means only that no matching record has been published here. If you find an error, please see [how to report a correction](/contribute/).
